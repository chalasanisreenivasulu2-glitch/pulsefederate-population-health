In Abu Dhabi, cardiovascular disease and diabetes account for a massive portion of mortality, driven by high rates of obesity and physical inactivity.

But hospitals cannot share the data needed to predict these risks... until now.

This is PulseFederate. A federated learning platform that brings the AI model to the data, instead of the data to the model.

Watch as three hospital sites train together. Site A and B are large urban hospitals. Site C is a smaller rural clinic.

Each site trains locally. Raw patient data never leaves the firewall. Only model weights are shared, protected by Differential Privacy.

Accuracy climbs as the federation learns. And notice the fairness score—it stays above ninety-seven percent. The global model does not leave the smaller rural clinic behind.

Finally, the Swagger API proves that every training round is programmatic, auditable, and ready for integration with Abu Dhabi's health systems.

Privacy and progress. Together.
